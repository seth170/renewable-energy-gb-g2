# Week 4: Member 3 – Distribution Histogram

## Interpretation of Actual Electricity Demand

The histogram shows an approximately symmetrical, unimodal distribution of actual electricity demand in Great Britain, with a very slight negative skewness of -0.0612. Most observations are concentrated around the central demand values, particularly between approximately 30,000 MW and 43,000 MW, with the highest frequency of 4,691 observations (9.31%) occurring within the 36,533.1–38,429 MW interval. The mean demand is 34,920.87 MW, slightly lower than the median of 35,306.50 MW, while the standard deviation of 7,888.62 MW indicates considerable variation in electricity demand. A few unusually low demand observations are also visible in the histogram.
